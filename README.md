This repository was created as part of the working paper "When Markets Shift: Detecting Multiple Structural Breaks in Financial Stress Indices" by Alberto Romero Aranda, Enrique Bátiz Zuk, and Jorge Solloa Cámara. The included video shows the shifting structural break dates for Model 4 by increasing the sample period progressively (30 observations at a time), which includes the FSIs of the US, 11 European countries, and China. Only the FSIs of the US, France, Germany, Great Britain and China are plotted.
